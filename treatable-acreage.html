DATA SOURCES — Geocoding: U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder. Parcel boundaries: Maricopa County Assessor's Office public GIS (auto-lookup currently covers Maricopa County only). Structure footprints: OpenStreetMap contributors, via Overpass API.
METHOD — Treatable area = parcel boundary area, minus the portion of any building/pool footprint that falls inside the parcel, minus manual exclusions entered above. Areas are computed directly from boundary coordinates, not from assessor-recorded acreage fields, so minor discrepancies vs. the tax record are expected. This is an estimating aid, not a survey — verify unusual or high-value lots on-site.
Outside Maricopa County, parcel auto-lookup isn't available yet — use manual entry with a measured or GIS-sourced lot size.
